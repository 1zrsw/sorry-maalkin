Pyaari Raani,

Maine jo kaha, wo mujhe nahi kehna chahiye tha. Mere shabdon ne tumhe hurt kiya, aur is baat ka mujhe sach mein bahut dukh hai. Main koi bahana nahi dunga. Galti meri thi.

Main tumhe Queen isliye nahi bulata tha ki ye bolne mein achha lagta hai. Main isliye bulata tha kyunki tum meri nazar mein sach mein wahi ho. Meri Maalkin, jiske saamne main apni saari akad bhool jaata hoon. Meri Raani, jiski ek muskaan se meri poori duniya halki ho jaati hai.

Tumhari hasi, tumhari baatein, tumhara andaaz, sab kuch mujhe bahut khaas lagta hai. Tum jaisi ho, wahi mujhe sabse zyada pasand ho. Aur ek raani ko apne logon se izzat aur pyaar milna chahiye, taana nahi. Ye baat maine apni galti se seekhi hai.

Tum jitna waqt chaho, le lo. Bas itna yaad rakhna ki tumhare liye meri izzat aur mera pyaar dono waise ke waise hain.

Tumhara,
